How to Join a Club

University clubs provide students with opportunities to explore their interests, develop new skills, and connect with fellow students. Follow these simple steps to become a member.

Registration Steps

01. Explore Clubs

Browse the available club categories and identify a club that matches your interests and hobbies.

02. Contact the Club

Reach out to the club representatives or visit their information desk to learn about membership requirements and registration.

03. Complete Registration

Follow the registration instructions, provide the required information, and confirm your membership.

Meeting Schedules

Clubs organize regular meetings, practice sessions, and activities throughout the academic year. Meeting days, times, and venues vary depending on the club.

Check club announcements or contact club representatives for the latest meeting schedules and event updates.

Participation Guidelines

  • Attend meetings and activities regularly.
  • Respect fellow members and their opinions.
  • Participate actively in club activities.
  • Work cooperatively with other members.
  • Follow club rules and university policies.
  • Take responsibility for assigned tasks.
